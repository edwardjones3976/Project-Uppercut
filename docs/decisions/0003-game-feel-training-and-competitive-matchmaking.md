# Decision 0003 — Game Feel, Player Development, and Competitive Matchmaking

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Supersedes:** Any wording that frames competitive play as protecting beginners or that isolates advanced players into a separate combat system

## Governing feel statement

> **Project Uppercut is arcade boxing with real boxing bones: instantly responsive, visually fast, genuinely funny, competitively fair, and much deeper than it initially appears.**

The production priority order is binding:

1. impressively responsive;
2. visually fast;
3. fun and funny;
4. accurate in boxing logic;
5. enjoyable across repeated matches;
6. steady-paced;
7. secretly layered with boxing idioms and advanced Ringcraft.

## Measurable definitions

### Impressively responsive

- Basic inputs register reliably and without unnecessary animation delay.
- Defense, movement, and counters respond within consistent authored windows.
- The player can understand when control begins, commits, and returns.
- A loss should not feel caused by the game responding late.

### Visually fast

- Animation, camera, effects, sound, crowd response, and commentary may create spectacle and speed.
- Mechanical windows remain readable and competitively fair.
- Spectators watching CPU vs. CPU should understand why major exchanges occurred.

### Fun and funny

- Immediate excitement comes from impact, personalities, entrances, reactions, commentary, crowd behavior, rivalries, and consequences.
- Humor supports rather than interrupts combat clarity.
- Comedy cannot require dishonest collisions, random outcomes, stereotypes, copied material, or mechanically meaningless chaos.

### Accurate

Accuracy means authentic boxing logic rather than strict visual simulation. Range, position, footwork, bodywork, repetition, counters, pressure, recovery, ring control, and composure must matter.

### Enjoyable

Enjoyment means sustained willingness to continue playing. Losses should be explainable, feedback should be useful, pacing should avoid exhaustion, and improvement should feel possible through learning and execution.

### Steady-paced

Fights should breathe through readable phases such as observation, engagement, exchange, reset, pressure, punishment, and momentum change. Constant visual noise, permanent stun pressure, and nonstop meter explosions are not acceptable pacing.

### Hidden boxing depth

Boxing idioms should become functional mechanics, CPU tendencies, training lessons, commentary, corner advice, and post-fight analysis. Players learn boxing logic through play rather than lecture.

## Player-development philosophy

> **Beginners receive preparation, not protection.**

The game does not lower competitive rules, hide assistance inside live matches, or suppress advanced mechanics. It develops beginners through a thought-provoking, detailed, easy-to-learn, fun-to-use, modern, useful, and reusable learning ecosystem.

Version 1 develops players primarily through:

- modular Training Mode;
- CPU sparring and Human vs. CPU;
- demonstrations, drills, immediate feedback, replay, and fight analysis.

Career Mode and Story Mode may later deepen beginner development, but they remain deferred to the next version under the accepted roadmap.

Head-to-head and online play activate the complete combat system. Competitive play must not include hidden beginner damage reduction, rubber-banding, score-driven comeback help, secret stamina assistance, selective outcome-changing input forgiveness, or restrictions placed on advanced knowledge.

## Competitive challenge distribution

Online matchmaking should provide a fair spread of challenges rather than permanently isolating players by skill.

- Beginners should not be abandoned to an endless sequence of hopeless matches.
- Advanced players should not be quarantined into fighting only advanced players.
- Players should encounter easier, comparable, and harder opposition over time.
- Matchmaking may reduce repeated extreme mismatches without changing combat rules or granting hidden advantages.
- Ranked, casual, open-challenge, event, and future tournament structures may use different search priorities, but the underlying combat system remains identical.
- The system should prevent deliberate, repeated beginner farming without treating every uneven matchup as invalid.

Advanced players earn success through knowledge, timing, spacing, mechanics, adaptation, matchup understanding, stamina management, and Ringcraft. Beginners are expected to learn those same systems through the game’s development tools.

## Consequences

- Replace “beginner protection” with “player preparation” throughout canonical documentation.
- Accessibility may improve perception, control consistency, and comprehension, but must not secretly alter competitive outcomes.
- Training and competition must never become incompatible games.
- Matchmaking quality should be evaluated by variety, rematch patterns, extreme-mismatch frequency, retention, and competitive integrity rather than by forcing every match toward equal ratings.
