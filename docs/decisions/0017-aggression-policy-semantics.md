# Decision 0017 — Aggression is initiative, not throw rate

**Status:** accepted; runtime implementation pending Phase S integration  
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Authority:** derives from Decision 0014, G3  
**Supersedes provisional branch numbering:** former local `0016-aggression-is-intent-not-throw-rate.md`

## Decision

`aggression_bias` is the tendency to seek offensive initiative when a plausible opportunity exists. It is not the probability that a decision tick becomes a punch.

The shared policy sequence is:

```text
observe → choose intent → test opportunity → choose action or observe → commit
```

Every attack still requires legal range, hand availability, stamina, recovery, a visible target lane, tactical value, repetition awareness, and acceptable positional risk.

## Role of the flat draw

The flat action draw remains authorized only as a deterministic null-hypothesis and path-stress harness. It is not a live CPU policy, balance evidence, or product behavior once the arena exists.

## Acceptance

The C++ policy emits reason codes, cannot read hidden scores or future actions, and produces purposeful pressure rather than authored punch spam.
