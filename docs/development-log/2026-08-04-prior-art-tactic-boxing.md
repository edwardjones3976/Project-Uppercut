# Prior-art intake — Tactic Boxing (Director-linked, 2026-08-04)

**What it is.** *Tactic Boxing* (Stately Eagle Games, $9.99, Steam release 2024, also
listed on the Epic Games Store): an indie boxing game with **active ragdoll physics**,
career mode, **manager mode**, quick fight, **AI-vs-AI spectating**, local multiplayer,
three style archetypes (Puncher, Slugger, Out-Boxer), nine development attributes
(strength, accuracy, health, stamina, footwork, attack speed, chin, HP/SP recovery),
40+ **non-licensed original boxers**, an in-game editor, and adjustable match settings
(damage, health, knockout chance). Steam reception: Mostly Positive (75% of ~377
reviews; recent trend higher). It is a GAME, not an asset — nothing here is a licensing
candidate, so the asset-compatibility audit does not apply; Rule 1's originality line
does: mechanics may be studied, nothing may be taken.

## What it validates for Project Uppercut

A one-person-scale indie at $9.99 holding Mostly Positive on exactly this recipe —
original no-license boxers, style archetypes, attribute-built fighters, career and
manager framing, and **AI-vs-AI as a first-class mode people actually watch** — is
market evidence for several of this project's standing commitments: CPU-vs-CPU as both
laboratory and product (PROJECT_RULES line 106), the stress-profile style system (six
named styles to their three), the budgeted attribute families (twenty-four to their
nine), mode-configurable match settings (`data/mode_configurations.json`), and the
original-roster legal posture. The appetite exists below the AAA licensed-boxing tier.

## Where the projects diverge, and why the divergence is the point

Tactic Boxing's combat truth is **active ragdoll** — outcomes emerge from physics, and
much of its charm is physics slapstick. Project Uppercut's combat truth is the opposite
commitment: deterministic, authored, replay-exact collision (Rule 13), byte-identical
resimulation, score isolation, and Rule 17's line that *comedy must never require
collision or outcome dishonesty* — the boxing stays competitively trustworthy while
the presentation is ridiculous, which is also what makes ranked play and networking
reachable. Ragdoll comedy is rented from the physics engine; Uppercut's comedy has to
be authored into personalities, timing and spectacle on top of honest boxing. Harder,
and the whole identity.

## Taken / not taken

- **Taken (concept level only):** confirmation that AI-vs-AI spectating deserves
  first-class presentation (camera, commentary hooks) rather than debug-view status;
  manager-mode framing as a proven wrapper for CPU-vs-CPU depth; adjustable
  match-settings surfaces as player-facing, not just config files.
- **Not taken:** any asset, name, character, branding, UI motif, or presentation
  element. Rule 1 and the originality gate apply to inspiration exactly as they do to
  marketplace content.

Filed as prior art beside the Ready 2 Rumble-inspired direction: near-neighbours in
premise, opposite in combat truth — a useful pole marking what Project Uppercut is
deliberately not.
