# Post-First-Bell readability and comprehension pilot

**Status:** Approved test plan; not canon evidence until run  
**Date:** 2026-08-08

This plan preserves the useful experimental part of the three-lens panel review without allowing hypothetical participants to dictate canon before a fight exists.

## Entry gate

Do not run this as a product judgment until:

- a complete integrated generic fight exists;
- the fight can reach at least one decision path and one stoppage path;
- the same authoritative runtime can be observed without developer-only reason text.

## Part A — guard readability

Use candidate player-facing guard families, not the entire research catalog by default.

Measure:

1. Static silhouette recognition.
2. “Where would you attack?” exposed-lane comprehension.
3. Motion/interception-frame recognition at real game speed.
4. Couch-distance readability over a real ring background.
5. Compressed-video readability.
6. Phone-sized viewport as a stress condition.
7. Response time and confusion matrix.

Include at least one older participant and at least one participant testing reduced visual clarity/corrected-vision stress.

The proposed `>20% pair confusion` collapse threshold is a hypothesis to evaluate, not a pre-approved canon number.

## Part B — does the fight explain itself without text?

Hide developer reason codes and telemetry.

After meaningful exchanges, knockdowns, recovery sequences and the final result, ask participants what they think happened.

Look for spontaneous explanations in families such as:

- tired/exhausted;
- trapped/cut off;
- could not get away;
- got caught while committed;
- body work accumulated;
- guard/defense failed on a side;
- opponent read the repetition.

The goal is not exact terminology. The goal is whether visible causality matches the authoritative cause.

## Part C — cultural/readability replication

Before broad launch testing, repeat Part B with boxing-aware participants from at least two different viewing/device contexts.

If the same authoritative event is consistently interpreted in incompatible ways, treat that first as a presentation/animation ambiguity to investigate rather than assuming one audience is wrong.

## Director boundary

This pilot may change:

- player-facing guard grouping;
- animation readability;
- camera framing;
- UI presentation;
- tutorial wording;
- audio/visual cues.

It does not automatically change:

- collision truth;
- scoring;
- Wind/Reserve authority;
- underlying guard geometry;
- punch commitment;
- CPU knowledge rules.
