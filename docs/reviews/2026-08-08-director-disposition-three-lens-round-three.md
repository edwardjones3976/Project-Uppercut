# Director disposition — Three-lens panel, round three

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Source:** `docs/reviews/2026-08-08-three-lens-panel-round-three.md`
**Authority:** This disposition, existing accepted decisions, and later superseding decisions. The panel itself remains a constructed review harness, not user research or gameplay evidence.

## Overall disposition

Round three is useful because it converges on **movement, pauses, commitment, and cadence** rather than proposing another broad subsystem. Its strongest contribution is not a new mechanic; it is a sequencing warning: do not treat cadence as meaningful while the bout still lacks authoritative evasion commitment, complete movement interaction, and consequence.

The project remains ordered toward First Bell. No panel ask may create a side quest that delays the complete fight unless it closes a defect in the active integrated path.

## Ask 1 — Two-contact chord cap

**Disposition: ACCEPTED AS AN INPUT CONTRACT CONSTRAINT; DEFER IMPLEMENTATION UNTIL HUMAN INPUT.**

Decision 0027 already establishes controller-first accessibility and rejects three-point competitive chords. The integrated simulation consumes **intentions**, not controller events. When the human input adapter is created, every legal base-game intention must be expressible without requiring more than two simultaneous contact points.

This does not enter the CPU-first First Bell critical path and does not require an input system now.

## Ask 2 — One-week disposition clock for exploratory-lab findings

**Disposition: REJECT THE CALENDAR DEADLINE; ACCEPT THE GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE.**

A fixed one-week clock is arbitrary and can reward shallow disposition merely to satisfy a date. The binding rule is instead:

> A lab finding that is used to propose canon, calibration, promotion to authoritative code, or a change to an active acceptance criterion must receive an explicit Director disposition **before that promotion or tuning occurs**.

Unanswered lab observations may remain observations. They may not silently become authority.

## Ask 3 — Close the evasion-recovery gap before cadence is trusted

**Disposition: ACCEPTED IMMEDIATELY.**

Decision 0032 now governs. The panel's wording that Python 'fails to carry' recovery is imprecise: Phase R's Python analyzer already reads authored startup/recovery and the mistime penalty. The actual missing piece is the First Bell integrated bout preserving evasion as a committed temporal action rather than a free tactical label.

No punch-rate, rest-rate, accessibility-cadence, or stamina-fit result becomes balance evidence until this commitment path is present.

## Ask 4 — Never show Crowd Bar and audience roar in the same frame

**Disposition: REJECTED AS WRITTEN; CAUSAL INDEPENDENCE REAFFIRMED.**

Decision 0028's split remains binding:

- Crowd Bar is a fighter gameplay resource.
- Audience Presentation State is presentation derived from observable boxing events.

The presentation system may **not** use Crowd Bar threshold crossings as causes for camera, replay, commentary, crowd swell, scoring, damage, stamina, contact, or knockdown behavior. Commentary may not explain a boxing event as caused by the Crowd Bar.

However, forbidding the Bar and a roar from appearing in the same frame is artificial. Independent systems are allowed to coincide. The test is causality, not visual superstition.

## Ask 5 — Vary judges on defence

**Disposition: DEFER TO SCORING INTEGRATION; ACCEPT AS A TEST QUESTION, NOT A DIRECT DEFENCE BONUS.**

Project Uppercut will not add a hidden standalone 'defence score' simply to make judges disagree. When sealed scoring enters the integrated bout, judge profiles must be reviewed for meaningful variation in how they interpret the boxing outcomes defence purchases: clean punching, effective aggression denied or created, ring generalship, positional control, and exchange quality.

The scoring review must test whether all judge profiles collapse to the same effective reading. If they do, that is a diversity/calibration defect to investigate. The fix must remain consistent with the project's scoring canon rather than inventing a bonus for avoiding punches.

## Ask 6 — Accessibility and tuning share a cadence question

**Disposition: ACCEPTED AS A POST-INTEGRATION EVALUATION RULE.**

Decision 0027's input floor is not sufficient if the authoritative fight eventually demands an unreasonable rate of intention formation. Therefore human-input accessibility review must include observed decision/action cadence.

No numeric cadence target is accepted from the panel. The current high punch-rate diagnostics are not fit evidence. Once movement, evasion commitment, contact, condition, and complete rounds coexist, cadence becomes a real Director/game-feel measurement.

## Round-three sequencing ruling

The panel's movement convergence does **not** authorize a new movement subsystem. The shared range core already exists and is now integrated through PR #27. The next work remains:

1. extract the minimal reusable Python Phase R seam;
2. re-prove historical Phase R byte-identically;
3. integrate active evasion commitment and recovery under Decision 0032;
4. preserve stage order: range/commitment → evasion → guard → contact;
5. then integrate contact quality and regional condition;
6. then knockdown/count/recovery/referee stoppage;
7. then sealed scoring/verdict;
8. then C++ integrated parity and Unreal arena parity;
9. then First Bell human readability/game-feel work.

## On the panel's 'movement and pauses' diagnosis

Accepted as a hypothesis worth testing, not as evidence. Boxing's readability often lives in the intervals between punches, but Project Uppercut has not yet produced a complete fight to measure that claim. The integrated runtime should now become capable of producing walking-down, denied exits, recovery attempts, slips, failed reads, trapped positioning, and disengagement. Then the Director watches the fight rather than tuning toward a panel's imagined version of it.

## Final Director position

Round three does not change the milestone.

`complete integrated fights = 0`

The next work must make evasion a real commitment inside the same fight that already owns policy, movement/range, and typed stamina. After that, punches may finally begin to have consequences worth scoring.
