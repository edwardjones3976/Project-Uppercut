# Director disposition — Three-lens panel review

**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Source:** `2026-08-08-three-lens-panel-review.md` supplied to the Director  
**Status:** Director disposition; the panel itself is not evidence

## Central judgment

The panel’s strongest finding is accepted: the project’s next strategic proof is still **one complete integrated fight**. The repository must not react to this review by opening a new design-program detour.

The panel is useful as a question generator. It is not a replacement for a build, player test, current canon or Director authority.

## Accepted now

1. **Complete fight remains first.** No new broad system outranks First Bell.
2. **Reason codes remain protected.** Observable-only CPU knowledge plus explainable reason codes are a high-leverage design asset.
3. **Wind over Reserve remains binding.** It is not traded away merely to shorten the default match.
4. **Punch commitment remains binding.** Input accessibility may improve intent recognition; it does not make live punches cancellable.
5. **Accessibility/input floor must be decided before human controls harden.** Decision 0027 does so without changing competitive authority.
6. **Comedy needs concrete scope.** Decision 0028 strengthens the dignity boundary and requires repeatable comedy grammar before named fighters.
7. **The recurring units defect is systemic.** Decision 0029 replaces naked live-runtime quantity boundaries with typed units.
8. **Post-First-Bell readability testing is worthwhile.** The guard/silhouette/comprehension pilot is accepted as a design test.
9. **Round-boundary persistence is useful for longer offline fights.** Deferred until the deterministic complete fight exists.

## Rejected or corrected

### Phone-first/platform compromise

Rejected as primary product direction.

The panel’s “frame for a phone, express on a television” compromise would let the hardest viewport dictate the core spatial game before evidence justifies that sacrifice. Project Uppercut depends on ring geography, local/couch readability, broadcast presentation and analog movement.

Decision 0027 sets PC/controller/TV-monitor framing as primary, while phone-sized viewing remains a stress test.

### Delete the Crowd Bar

Rejected.

The Crowd Bar is established canon with explicit competitive boundaries. The useful insight beneath the panel recommendation is retained by creating a **separate presentation-only audience reaction layer**. Decision 0028 preserves both concepts without conflating them.

### Collapse ten guards immediately

Rejected as premature.

The ten researched guards are not ten required buttons. Their eventual player-facing grouping is a readability/input question. The research catalog stays; the direct control surface is tested after First Bell and before Human-vs-CPU.

### Demote byte identity to release-only

Rejected for authoritative cross-language simulation changes.

Exploratory Python-only labs are allowed and may accelerate observation, but they remain explicitly non-authoritative until parity is proven. Decision 0028 records that boundary.

### Shorten competitive recovery/startup as accessibility

Rejected.

Accessibility may make the input easier to express, latch, remap or buffer. It does not secretly alter authoritative competitive frame data. Decision 0027 formalizes the distinction.

## Deferred until there is a fight to observe

- exact player-facing guard count;
- actual silhouette confusion threshold;
- whether Reserve/Wind animation communicates without text;
- comedy timing quality;
- camera comprehension;
- crowd readability;
- platform expansion beyond PC/controller-first;
- final UI density;
- long-fight save/resume implementation.

These are not ignored. They are deliberately sequenced behind the artifact required to test them.

## Director takeaway

The review correctly sees a risk: the project can become a brilliant boxing kernel that postpones the videogame indefinitely.

The answer is **not** to replace carefully established mechanics with hypothetical market simplifications before anybody has played the game.

The answer is:

```text
finish First Bell
→ watch it
→ test readability and comprehension
→ expose only the depth that survives contact with players
→ keep deeper internal boxing truth where it adds causality
```

> **The panel is allowed to challenge the painting. It does not get to repaint the canvas before the first image exists.**
