# Three-lens panel, round three — the same reviewers on the revisions

**Date:** 2026-08-08 · **Requested by:** the Director · **Addendum to:**
`2026-08-08-three-lens-panel-review.md` and its disposition
(`2026-08-08-director-disposition-three-lens-panel.md`)

**The same harness disclaimer applies and is not repeated in full: this is a
constructed review device, not user research, and nothing in it is evidence.**
The same three constructed reviewers — same transcripts continued, so each
argues from what they already said — were given the Director's rulings on their
own recommendations (Decisions 0027–0031) and the work built since (typed
quantities, the defensive-purity correction, evasion in the integrated bout,
the first cadence findings), and asked to review the revisions with their own
positions on the line.

What is different about this round: two of three panellists ruled **against
their own earlier positions** when the evidence asked them to, and the round's
sharpest findings are about sequencing, not design.

## 1. How each took losing

**Adaeze Nwankwo** (lost the platform argument): *"I lost, and I will not
pretend the ruling is foolish… the honest part of me that runs brackets on
borrowed monitors knows a PC build reaches my cafés faster than a Play Store
listing reaches my aunties."* But she names the failure mode: *"'phone as
torture test' is how mobile dies politely. Torture tests get skipped the first
week a milestone slips."* Her single demand, priced deliberately small — see §3.

**Kenji Muraoka** (lost the byte-parity demotion AND the asymmetric commit
window): concedes both, the second against himself in terms: *"My asymmetric
recovery window did falsify the punch — I dressed a game-state change as an
input courtesy and the Director caught the seam. 'Forgive the hand where
possible; do not falsify the punch' is the honest version of what I actually
wanted."* On the exploratory lab: *"I asked him to demote a rule, and instead
he built a second room where my loop can run without the rule pretending not to
exist."*

**Rocío Salazar-Ibáñez** (lost the Crowd Bar kill, won the split; and was the
panellist who had praised the retired "anti-running rule"): *"I praised a
comment, not a mechanic, and I am glad to be corrected — this is the process
working, and note that it was a reading pass that caught it, not a test."* On
the new permanent sentence she goes further than endorsement, into the boxing:
*"Judges do not score defence. They score what defence purchases… my father
never applauded a clean getaway, he applauded the counter that followed it."*

## 2. The round's convergence: all three findings point at movement

Round one converged on "run one fight." Round three converges on something
narrower, and all three arrived at it from different evidence:

- **Rocío, on the broadcast:** the unreachable `region` word costs nothing
  today and 200 punches is a tuning dial — *"The fact that damages the
  broadcast most is the footwork… Boxing is watchable between punches; the
  walk-down, the give of ground, the ropes arriving — that is the picture, and
  without it the reason-code stream narrates a slot machine."* Her minimum for
  a callable fight: *"one fighter visibly walks the other to the ropes, the
  trapped man pays for being there, and one exchange ends because somebody
  left."*
- **Adaeze, on the teaching layer she championed:** *"Reason codes teach only
  in negative space: RECOVERY_DENIED means something when recovery was visibly
  attempted, and at 200 punches a round there is no visible anything — the
  stream becomes static… the model does not yet fear, and fear is the pause,
  and the pause is where both the lesson and the punchline live."* She also
  turns the project's own emergence rule on it: if body shots beat every
  authored evasion so hard that `region` cannot occur, *"the zone values are
  wrong, by canon, no debate needed."*
- **Kenji, on the optimizer:** the named evasion-recovery gap is not cosmetic.
  *"A defensive action whose failure costs only whatever happens to land
  during it is free whenever the opponent is not mid-punch — so the optimal
  line is to twitch through every telegraph… which means reads stop paying,
  which is the game again. My Osaka regulars would find this line inside two
  weeks."*

**The composite claim, stated once:** the fight currently punches too much
because nothing makes it move, wait, or fear — and the three assets the panel
called unique (the reason codes, the ring, the telegraphs) are all stories
about movement and pauses that the cadence does not yet contain.

## 3. Specific asks, each priced by its asker

1. **Adaeze — the chord cap, now, while the input layer is vapour.** *"The
   simulation must consume intentions, not controller events, and the
   intention vocabulary must carry a written chord cap — no legal input
   requires more than two simultaneous contact points. That is one interface
   file and one sentence of canon."* She notes Decision 0027's own
   no-three-point-chord line already implies it; this writes it where the
   integrated contract can enforce it. *"If the chord cap is refused, then the
   torture test was always a eulogy."*
2. **Kenji — the rule that keeps the exploratory lab alive:** *"Every lab run
   ends with a numbered observation, and every observation receives a written
   disposition — adopted or refused — from the authoritative side within one
   week. Findings must flow uphill on a clock even though code never does. The
   moment a lab finding can sit unanswered, the lab is furniture."*
3. **Kenji — close the evasion-recovery gap before trusting any cadence
   number:** *"data gathered in a world where defence is free will mis-teach
   every tuner who reads it — but it need not delay First Bell, because the
   record shows it is plumbing, not design: canon and the C++ side already
   author the recovery; the Python pack simply fails to carry it. A named gap
   this cheap should not survive the week."*
4. **Rocío — the broadcast fence around the Crowd Bar split:** never the Bar
   and the roar in the same frame at the moment of an exchange; no camera,
   replay or commentary trigger keyed to the Bar crossing a threshold —
   *"presentation reacts to the boxing, on its own logic, even when that means
   the room goes quiet while the Bar is full"*; commentary never speaks the
   Bar as a cause. Show the Bar in the player-HUD grammar where Wind lives.
   *"If the two systems ever fire in visible lockstep, the split exists only
   in the repo."*
5. **Rocío — vary the judges on defence.** *"If all seven scoring models read
   defence identically, the game under-scores the master everywhere instead of
   somewhere, and part of boxing's truth is that Judge A gives Whitaker the
   round and Judge B does not."*
6. **Kenji — accessibility and tuning are now one question.** The adopted
   floor solves entering an intention, not the rate at which intentions must
   be formed: *"Hold cadence to boxing's numbers and the floor suffices; let
   it drift toward 146 and Decision 0027 becomes a beautifully worded fence."*

## 4. On the comedy carve-out

Adaeze, who authored the couch rule, accepts the Director's trim against her
earlier position: *"the premature pose passes my rule… My rule was never that a
fighter cannot fail funnily; it was that a fighter cannot be the failure. The
pose is a choice, made in hubris, punished by physics."* The line she holds:
*"between what a fighter does and what a fighter is."* She also flags, half
seriously, that the mutual-punish deadlock — two men reading each other as
permanently open, 146 punches a minute, not one step — *"is honestly the
funniest thing this project has produced, which tells you the physics can
already generate comedy before anyone writes a joke."*

## 5. Week-after-First-Bell, one sentence each

- **Adaeze:** the ugly fight on a television and a phone in front of ten
  strangers, letting what they laugh at and squint at decide the readability
  pilot and the guard count together.
- **Kenji:** the silhouette deck for seven minutes, then the fight with one
  question at each stoppage — "what just happened to him?" — *"and my eyes on
  their eyes, not the screen."*
- **Rocío:** *"build the corner — sixty seconds between rounds where fatigue,
  damage and advice become visible, because that is where a fight becomes a
  story."*

## 6. What this round cannot judge

Unchanged from round one and restated because it is truer now: nobody,
including these three, has watched a fight, because none exists. Every claim
above about how the bout *plays* extrapolates from stream counts and reason
histograms. The number is still **0**, and the panel's standing instruction —
finish the fight, then let humans attack it — is unaltered by anything here.
