# Phase 0 Gate Item — Character Vertical-Slice Acceptance Criteria (draft for Director approval)

Approved **separately** from the sandbox criteria, and applied only **after** the sandbox gate passes.
The pairing is Adewale "The Fine Print" Balogun and Calvin "The Host" Givens. Character identity,
personality, appearance, background and dialogue are Director-owned content; this document specifies
the acceptance bar, not the characters.

## Entry condition

The generic sandbox gate has passed. Character work does not begin before it.

## Completeness — each of the two fighters

1. Original identity, visual design, background, personality, entrance and rivalry hooks.
2. A stated primary stance and authored stance familiarity.
3. Authored attributes passing the Rule 10 validator with an approved budget formula.
4. At least fifteen meaningful traits from the project's training and trait vocabulary.
5. Exactly two counterable Specials.
6. Exactly one powerful but counterable Ultimate that cannot automatically end a match.
7. Exactly one distinctive Full-Crowd Trait.
8. Visible vulnerabilities that preserve matchup counterplay.

## What the pairing must prove

9. **Balogun** exercises timing, Ringcraft, range structure, counters, pattern recognition, and
   disciplined pressure and adaptation.
10. **Givens** exercises speed, combinations, Showmanship, evasive exits, crowd generation and rhythm
    disruption.

## Gate

11. Both fighters remain distinct **without requiring different underlying combat rules**.
12. Character-specific behaviour does not conceal generic system faults — a fault reproducible with
    generic fixtures is a sandbox fault and returns there.
13. One full Crowd Bar creates a choice between Ultimate and Full-Crowd Trait, never both.
14. Neither fighter creates unavoidable ability sequences, hidden stat boosts or automatic match endings.
15. Simulation reports explain the pair's tactical differences and recurring failure modes.
16. Trait duplication between the two remains limited enough that each stays mechanically and personally
    distinct, without relying on obscenity, caricature, intentional ugliness or random weirdness.
